\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:intro}
Software forms the backbone of modern society, supporting critical infrastructure such as communication networks, financial systems, and healthcare services. The security of these systems is a pressing concern, as vulnerabilities can lead to severe consequences, including financial losses, reputational harm, and even threats to human safety. Consequently, society has a significant indirect interest in software security due to its heavy reliance on digital infrastructure. \cite{Wilson_2020}

Beyond societal concerns, software vendors also have compelling interests in providing secure software. On the one hand, companies can financially benefit from voluntarily disclosing their security measures. This transparency and proactive approach can positively influence their market value. \cite{Gordon_2010}

On the other hand, software vendors are mandated to comply with various data protection laws and regulations. Non-compliance can result in severe penalties, including fines of up to 20,000,000€ limited to 4\% of their total worldwide annual turnover. \cite{GDPR}

\section{Problem Statement}
\label{sec:intro:problem_statement}
Cyber security requirements need to be met while surrounded by a dynamically and fast evolving threat landscape. State and non-state actors drive a wide and diverse landscape of cyber security threats. Supply chain attacks, advanced persistent threats and vulnerabilities on the Internet of Things are just some cyber security threats that modern systems face. \cite{Adeyeri_2024} More dynamic and scalable security solutions than traditional methods are needed to prevent data breaches and exploitable vulnerabilities. \cite{Stefinko_2024}

\section{Goal of research}
\label{sec:intro:goal}
% todo: quellen mit 3 measures of software security alignen
To address growing difficulties of securing software systems, corporate vendors and developers can apply several security measures. The \ac{SDLC} describes multiple steps in which the development of secure software can be split up. One central part of the development of secure software is testing. \cite{McGraw_2004} A highly effective approach of software testing is penetration testing \cite{Lachkov_2022}. Penetration testing used to be a manual and creative approach of dynamically testing for security vulnerabilities in software. However, traditional and manual methods of penetration testing are no longer sufficient to keep track of fast evolving threat landscapes and ever growing software systems. This raises the need for automated penetration testing. \cite{Stefinko_2024} The goal of this term paper is to determine the extent to which penetration testing can be automated by leveraging \ac{LLM}s.

\section{Research Question and Hypotheses}
\label{sec:intro:research_question}
The central research question addressed in this term paper is following: RQ: Are \ac{LLM}s capable of performing automated penetration tests in corporate environments?\\

\noindent The hypotheses to answer this question is following: H: \ac{LLM}s are capable of performing automated penetration tests on corporate and proprietary applications.

\section{Structure}
\label{sec:intro:structure}
The main content of this term paper will consist of three parts. To emphasize the role of penetration testing in secure software development, the first step will differentiate between measures of software security. The second step will introduce penetration testing to answer the research question. The third step will examine \ac{AI}-driven penetration testing. The findings of these three main parts will be carefully discussed. In the conclusion, all information will be summarized and synthesized. The hypotheses will also be evaluated based on these findings.

\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:intro:methodology}
A literature review will discuss past and ongoing research on the automation of penetration testing. Capabilities of earlier solutions will be compared to the current state of research. This term paper will partially be based on research papers that are still in pre-print or have not received much attention yet because they have been published recently. Findings of pre-print papers will be carefully discussed in this term paper to ensure quality. Because the technology of \ac{AI} and especially \ac{LLM}s is still evolving and receives broad attention, some aspects might change in the future. Special attention will be drawn to security experts' reports of experience. Corporate security experts actually working with \ac{AI} driven penetration testing tools which is why their experiences cover the most recent evolutions. Goals for future research will be defined after consulting and discussing literature and experts' experiences.
