\chapter{State of Research and related Work}
\label{ch:foundations_related_work}

\noindent Penetration tests can be performed with three different knowledge levels: Black-Box penetration testing means that the penetration tester has no internal knowledge of the system under test. White-Box Testing means the Tester has complete knowledge of the internals and Grey-Box refers to partial knowledge of internal structures. \cite{Rane_2024}

Tools like BurpSuite, ZAP and SQLMap already implement automated penetration testing techniques through signature-driven \ac{DAST} scanning approaches. These scanners systematically map web applications, fuzz parameters with curated payloads, and detect injection vulnerabilities like XSS, SQLi, and command injection. However, these approaches are fundamentally constrained by their pattern-matching limitations that prevent the contextual reasoning, multi-step exploitation chains, and novel attack creativity required to identify complex business logic vulnerabilities and zero-day exploits. \cite{Osman_2025}

Earlier approaches to automate the process of penetration testing leveraged \ac{AI} models trained through Reinforcement Learning \cite{Confido_2022}. Training environments for these models need to be high fidelity which makes the models inflexible. The lack of flexibility brought up difficulties scaling \ac{AI} models trained through Reinforcement Learning to an production ready state. \cite{Schwartz_2019}

Leveraging \ac{LLM}s for penetration testing shows better results and scalability. \ac{LLM}s are able to apply attach patterns to applications more flexible. The project PentestGPT describes an agentic approach for orchestrating \ac{LLM}s beneficially which further increases the performance. \cite{Deng_2024}

Little research has been conducted on the ability of \ac{LLM}s to automatically perform penetration tests on corporate and proprietary applications. This term paper will investigate whether \ac{LLM}s are able to perform automated penetration tests in corporate environments.
